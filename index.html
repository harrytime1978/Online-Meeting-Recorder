About transcription
Speaker labeling currently uses gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize (deprecated; scheduled shutdown February 26, 2027). Long recordings are automatically compressed and split into approximately eight-minute audio pieces for processing. Speaker numbering can restart between pieces. Identification is by anonymous speaker labels, not verified real-world identities. Summaries and translations are generated after transcription.
